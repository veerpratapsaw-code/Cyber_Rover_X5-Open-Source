   • Dual-Radio Hybrid Architecture: Both radios run together on the STM32. NRF24 drives;

     falls back to LoRa through concrete walls.

     - SPDT 3-Position sequential switch (Pin 1 to Pin 2 / Pin 1 to Pin 3 / Both Open) uses exactly 2 GPIO pins (Pin 1 to GND, Pins 2 & 3 to STM32 pins).
     - Momentary push button uses 1 GPIO pin (Pin 1 to GND, Pin 2 to STM32 pin).

Step 2.12: Receiver RF Link Bench Verification & Silicon Disconnect Diagnosis (Firmware v1.2): [COMPLETED & VERIFIED]
  - Live RF Link Bench Test Results:
    * Transmitter confirmed 100% operational: Frame stream verified with active stick movement, potentiometer changes, and live push button presses (PL:1, PR:1 successfully registered on RX).
    * Bench Hardware Anomaly Identified: Receiver logs showed periodic "NRF24 SILICON DISCONNECTED!" and bursts of corrupted 0xFFFF (65535) frames registering at 4000+ Hz.
  - Root Cause Analysis:
    1. Floating SPI Bus: Loose DuPont jumpers or female socket on PA3 (CE), PA4 (CSN), PA5 (SCK), PA6 (MISO), PA7 (MOSI) cause SPI lines to float HIGH (0xFF), causing RF24 driver to report false packet-ready interrupts.
    2. Power Rail Collapse / Decoupling: NRF24L01+PA+LNA power spikes during receive cause voltage brownouts on 3.3V rail if lacking a 10uF-100uF capacitor directly across NRF24 VCC and GND pins, or if receiver Blue Pill is powered from weak USB-TTL 3.3V LDO.
  - Receiver Firmware Hardening (cyberrover_x5_rover_rx.ino v1.2.1):
    1. 12-Bit ADC Packet Sanity Check: All 7 analog values are validated `<= 4095`. Any corrupted 0xFFFF frames are immediately flushed from RX FIFO and rejected.
    2. Zero SPI Register Contention: Removed active `isChipConnected()` polling from the primary packet loop. During active packet reception, `radio.available()` is processed directly with zero SPI register interruption, eliminating false disconnect alerts.
    3. Failsafe-Only Auto-Recovery: Silicon probe and re-initialization are only performed if the link has dropped for > 2000 ms.
