## 🌟 1. What is CyberRover X5?

CyberRover X5 is a heavy-duty, fast robot ground vehicle:
* **Speed:** Zooms at almost **$12\text{ km/h}$** (super fast for a ground rover!).
* **Drifting & Stunts:** Can do $360^\circ$ tank spins, power drifts, climb steep ramps, and jump off ledges.
* **Super Strong (RoboFight Monster):** Weighs $2.4\text{ kg}$ with heavy steel brackets and 4 powerful motors. In a robot fight, it can easily push obstacles and other robots weighing over $10\text{ to } 15\text{ kg}$!
* **Wireless Screen Remote:** Has a cool handheld remote with an OLED mini-TV screen that shows live battery power and car speed!

## 🛠️ 2. The 4 Basic Steps to Build It

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      HOW THE ROVER COMES TOGETHER                      │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. THE BODY (CHASSIS)                                                  │
│    Cut two pieces of plywood with a hand saw. Separate them with       │
│    4 long threaded steel screws to make a two-floor sandwich.          │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. MOTORS & TIRES                                                      │
│    Screw 4x Johnson 12V 600 RPM geared motors to the bottom wood       │
│    using steel brackets. Attach four big 100mm rubber wheels.          │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. THE BRAIN & BATTERY                                                 │
│    Place a BTS7960 motor driver board, an STM32 Blue Pill microchip,   │
│    and an ESP32 wireless chip on top. Plug in a 12V or 3S drone LiPo.  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. THE REMOTE TRANSMITTER                                              │
│    Make a small box out of thin MDF craft wood. Add two thumb          │
│    joysticks, 3 volume-style knobs, switches, and a mini OLED screen.  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 🧠 3. Your Secret Superpower: Using Agentic AI

You don't need to be a software genius or memorize thousands of lines of C++ code.  
**Veer built this entire rover by working with an "Agentic AI"!**

### What is an "Agentic AI"?
Normal AI only answers simple text questions.  
An **Agentic AI** (like Google Antigravity, Cursor, or Windsurf) is like an **expert robotics engineer sitting next to you at your computer desk**. It can:
* Read every blueprint and wiring diagram in this folder.
* Check your code for bugs and fix errors automatically.
* Tell you which wire goes where in plain English.
* Guide you step-by-step so you never feel lost!

## 🏆 6. Why This Robot Wins in School RoboFights

1. **Heavyweight Grip:** Weighing $2.4\text{ kg}$ on wide 100mm rubber tires, light plastic toy cars cannot push it.
2. **Brutal Motor Power:** 4x Johnson 600 RPM motors give **$97.6\text{ Newtons}$ of push force**. It can push rolling objects up to $20\text{ kg}$!
3. **Steel Brackets:** If other robots ram into the wheels, the heavy steel EasyMech motor mounts absorb the shock without breaking.
4. **Instant Acceleration:** It zooms from 0 to full speed in $0.26$ seconds, so you can dodge enemy robots or ram them at full speed!

## ⚠️ 7. Student Safety Rules (Important!)

* **Ask an Adult or Teacher:** When using a hacksaw, utility knife, or hot soldering iron, always have a teacher or parent nearby to supervise.
* **Battery Care:** Treat drone LiPo batteries with respect. Never puncture them, never short red (+) to black (-), and charge them using an authentic LiPo balance charger (like B3 or B6).
